Advanced Solutions For Challenging Cargo

Unconventional loads need more than a truck

Anything that exceeds legal length, width, height, or weight stops being a trucking problem and becomes an engineering problem. Our transport team builds the route before the load ever leaves the yard — measuring clearances, checking bridge ratings, sequencing escorts, and matching the trailer configuration to the cargo rather than forcing the cargo onto whatever trailer is available.

Superload & multi-axle transport

Loads that exceed routine permit thresholds and require engineered routing, axle-weight distribution, and state-level superload approval.

Crane & heavy equipment relocation

Crawler cranes, boom sections, counterweights, excavators, and pile rigs moved between jobsites, yards, and ports.

Over-dimensional freight

Tanks, vessels, girders, generators, transformers, modular structures, and precast that will not fit inside legal envelopes.

Port, rail, and yard drayage

Heavy cargo moved off the dock or railhead and onto the jobsite, including staging and interim storage.

Route surveys & lift planning

Pre-trip surveys, clearance verification, and coordinated lift plans so the crane, the trailer, and the crew arrive matched to the job.

Project & multi-load moves

Phased transport for plant relocations, bridge components, and construction programs that ship over weeks rather than days.

Fleet & Trailer Configurations

The right deck for the load

Trailer selection drives everything downstream — permit class, route options, escort requirements, and cost. We spec the configuration to the dimensions and weight of your cargo.

Flatbed

Standard legal freight

Steel, pipe, machinery, and material within legal length and height.

Step deck & drop deck

Added height clearance

Taller cargo that would exceed height limits riding on a standard flatbed.

Double drop & extendable

Long, tall loads

Well-deck trailers that stretch for girders, blades, and oversized fabrications.

RGN / lowboy

Drive-on, drive-off

Removable gooseneck decks for tracked and wheeled equipment loaded from the front.

Multi-axle heavy haul

Weight distribution

Axle counts scaled to spread gross weight within legal per-axle limits on permitted routes.

Specialized & dual-lane

Superload class

Engineered configurations, jeeps and boosters, and dual-lane setups for the widest and heaviest cargo.

How It Works

From dimensions to delivered

  1. 1

    Send the load details

    Dimensions, weight, pick-up and delivery points, and any site constraints. Photos and drawings help.

  2. 2

    We survey and spec

    Trailer configuration, route survey, clearance and bridge checks, and escort requirements are worked out and priced.

  3. 3

    Permits and scheduling

    We pull the permits, book escorts and any utility work, and lock a travel window that fits your schedule.

  4. 4

    Load, haul, set

    Our crews load the trailer, run the route, and — if you need it — bring the crane to set the load in place on arrival.

Heavy haul drivers wanted

We're looking for CDL drivers who already know what it takes to run oversize — drivers who can read a permit, hold a line through a tight turn, and secure a load they'd be comfortable following down the interstate. If that's you, Sims Crane has steady, home-based work across 16 Florida and Georgia branches.

What we're looking for
